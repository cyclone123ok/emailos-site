EmailOS privacy policy

EmailOS keeps your mail on your own computer. It has no company server, no advertising, and no analytics.

Last updated: 6 November 2026

1. What EmailOS reads

EmailOS reads the mailboxes you connect. It uses two Google permissions:

  • gmail.modify lets it read, compose, send, label, and move mail to Trash
  • gmail.settings.basic lets it create filters in Gmail when you ask

EmailOS has no function that deletes mail for good. Its delete action moves mail to your provider's Trash folder. Your provider decides when to empty that folder.

2. Where your data lives

EmailOS stores your mail, categories, and history in an encrypted database on your computer. Your sign-in tokens and the database key sit in Windows Credential Manager. The developer has no server and receives no copy of your data.

Attachments you save and files you export are ordinary files. They sit unencrypted in the folder you choose, and EmailOS does not remove them.

EmailOS keeps local mail copies until you remove them, or until they pass a retention period you set in Settings.

3. When data leaves your computer

EmailOS talks to your mail provider to fetch, change, and send mail. Data reaches anyone else only in these four cases, each of which you switch on or start:

  • Anthropic (AI reading). With your own API key, EmailOS sends email text to the Anthropic API to sort mail and draft replies. Anthropic handles that text under its API terms.
  • Have I Been Pwned (breach check). With your own API key, EmailOS sends your connected email addresses to this service when you run a check. Breach data comes from Have I Been Pwned under a CC BY 4.0 licence.
  • Webhooks. If you add a webhook address, EmailOS sends event details to that address.
  • One-click unsubscribe. When you unsubscribe, EmailOS contacts the address the sender provides for that purpose.

EmailOS keeps three kinds of mail away from the cloud AI: encrypted mail, mail from senders you mark as local only, and mail in categories you mark as local only. Without an Anthropic key, a basic reader sorts your mail on your computer.

The email you read and your messages can hold other people's personal information. If you use AI reading, that information goes to Anthropic with the email.

4. Google user data

EmailOS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • EmailOS uses Gmail data only to provide the features you use
  • EmailOS does not sell Gmail data
  • EmailOS does not use Gmail data for advertising
  • EmailOS does not use Gmail data to train general AI models
  • No person reads your Gmail data, unless you ask for help and share it yourself

5. Removing your data

  • In EmailOS, open Settings and remove an account. This deletes its stored sign-in and its local mail copies.
  • In EmailOS, use the erase option in Settings to delete local copies of mail, drafts, and history. Your sender marks stay, so a local-only mark keeps working. Saved attachments and exports stay in your folders.
  • In your Google Account, open Third-party connections to remove the app's access.
