EmailOS terms of use

EmailOS is a personal project. It is free, and it is not for sale.

Last updated: 7 November 2026

1. Who may use it

EmailOS has no public sign-up. Its developer and people the developer knows personally may use it.

2. Your responsibilities

  • Connect only mailboxes you are allowed to use
  • Follow the law when you send mail, including New Zealand spam law
  • Follow the terms of your mail provider and any AI service you connect
  • You are responsible for each auto-send rule and away message. EmailOS sends them without further review
  • Check AI summaries and drafts before you rely on them

3. Marketing mail

EmailOS has no unsubscribe system. Do not send marketing with it, including in a single email, a signature, or an auto-reply, unless you meet the spam law yourself. A message with a commercial link counts. The law requires consent, your contact details, and a working unsubscribe.

4. No promise of service

EmailOS is an unfinished project, offered as it is. It can contain errors. Keep your own copies of anything important.

5. Governing law

New Zealand law governs these terms.
