Mirage

Less inbox. More day.

Mirage is a personal email app that runs on your own computer.

See what Mirage does

Email sorted into categories you choose

  • Sorts incoming mail into categories you choose
  • Flags mail that needs a reply
  • Archives, labels, and moves mail in bulk
  • Moves unwanted mail to your provider's Trash folder, which your provider empties on its own schedule

Replies you check before they go

Mirage drafts replies for you to review. Nothing an AI drafts goes out until you approve it.

AI summaries and drafts can be wrong, incomplete, or out of date. Check them before you rely on them.

The spelling and grammar check runs on your computer.

Automatic replies that wait

A rule can answer a kind of email with words you wrote. You choose how long it waits, from minutes to seven days.

If you reply first, it stays unsent. Rules that send on their own never depend on an AI's choice.

Your data

Mirage stores its copy of your mail on your own computer. It asks for access to your Gmail so it can read, sort, label, move, and send mail for you, and create Gmail filters when you ask. It uses that access only for these features.

The privacy policy names each outside service it can use.

Who it is for

Mirage is a personal project. It has no public sign-up and no paid plan.
