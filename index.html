EmailOS

EmailOS is a personal email app that runs on your own computer.

Google's sign-in screen shows the app under the name inbox-sorter.

What it does

  • Sorts incoming mail into categories you choose
  • Flags mail that needs a reply
  • Drafts replies for you to review
  • Archives, labels, and moves mail in bulk
  • Moves unwanted mail to your provider's Trash folder, which your provider empties on its own schedule

Who it is for

EmailOS is a personal project. It has no public sign-up and no paid plan.

Your data

EmailOS keeps your mail on your computer. The privacy policy names each outside service it can use.

AI output

AI summaries and drafts can be wrong, incomplete, or out of date. Check them before you rely on them. Nothing an AI drafts goes out until you approve it.
